# WoobleSpritePack 🎞️

*Animated, true-colour sprites for all 1025 Pokémon, from Red to Emerald.*

</div>

A [Gen1Recomp](https://github.com/bryanthaboi/gen1recomp) mod for Red, Blue, Yellow, Gold,
Silver, Crystal, FireRed, LeafGreen, Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald. Based on
[g9-battle-sprites](https://github.com/tectorifter/g9-battle-sprites), which it replaces on
Red to Crystal.

## ✨ Features

### Core Mechanics

- 🎞️ **Every species animated:** front and back sprites for #1–1025, normal and shiny, in
  battle, the Pokédex and the summary screen.
- 🌍 **Forms included:** regional and alternate forms, female looks, and all 28 Unown letters.
- 🎒 **Party icons:** for every Pokémon on Red to Crystal, and for #387 onward on FireRed to
  Emerald.
- 🔄 **Face-to-face battles:** in every game, your Pokémon can show its front instead of its
  back, facing you or turned toward the foe (3DB FRONTAL and 3DB FLIP).
- 📝 **Save editor:** Gen1Recomp's save editor shows the same art.

### FireRed to Emerald

- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Animated trainers:** the trainers you battle are drawn in a modern style and play
  their own animation once they've slid in. That covers nearly every Kanto trainer and Hoenn's gym
  leaders, Steven, Wally and the common classes.
- ⚡ **VS screens:** Kanto's gym leaders, Giovanni, your rival, Bruno and Lance open their battles
  with an HGSS-style VS screen that fills the whole window.

### Red to Crystal

- 🎮 **Every screen:** evolutions, trades, the Hall of Fame, Prof. Oak's speech and the other
  screens that show a Pokémon use the art too.
- 🌗 **Shadows and floaters:** Pokémon cast a soft shadow in battle, and flyers hover.

### Looks Right

- 📐 **Framed like the originals:** sprites sit where the game's own would, and big Pokémon are
  shown whole.
- 🖥️ **HD with [WoobleView](https://github.com/poooooby/gen3-hd-sprites):** sprites are drawn
  crisply at your screen's resolution and sized by how tall each Pokémon really stands, measured
  from its own art (a long one like Gyarados by how high it rears, not its length nose to tail).
- 📱 **Phone-friendly:** small enough for Android.

## ⚙️ Options

Four options work in every game and one is for FireRed to Emerald only; the rest are for Red to
Crystal, since FireRed to Emerald follow the game's own layout (the in-game Mod Manager shows
only the five there).

| Option | Games | Choices | Default |
|---|---|---|---|
| BATTLE SPRITES | all | on / off | on |
| ANIMATE | all | on / off | on |
| 3DB FRONTAL (your Pokémon faces you) | all | on / off | off |
| 3DB FLIP (...and faces the foe) | all | on / off | off |
| ANIMATED TRAINERS (and VS screens) | FireRed to Emerald | on / off | on |
| SPRITE SPEED | Red to Crystal | slow / normal / fast / very fast | normal |
| SHINY ART | Red to Crystal | on / off | on |
| FEMALE ART | Red to Crystal | on / off | on |
| SPRITE SIZE | Red to Crystal | max detail / small / normal / big | max detail |
| GROUND ANCHOR | Red to Crystal | feet / pack / box | feet |
| FLOATERS | Red to Crystal | on / off | on |
| BATTLE SHADOWS | Red to Crystal | on / off | on |
| DEX SPRITES | Red to Crystal | on / off | on |
| SUMMARY SPRITES | Red to Crystal | on / off | on |
| SUMMARY SIZE | Red to Crystal | real size / 75% / 50% / 25% | real size |
| OTHER SCREENS | Red to Crystal | on / off | on |
| PARTY ICONS | Red to Crystal | on / off | on |

## 💾 Installation

1. FireRed to Emerald: install [WoobleDex](https://github.com/poooooby/national_dex_gen3)
   (required). Red to Crystal: turn off G9 Battle Sprites.
2. Download the latest [release](https://github.com/poooooby/g9-battle-sprites-gen3/releases).
3. Import it with the Gen1Recomp Mod Manager and enable it.

## 🔗 Works With

- [WoobleDex](https://github.com/poooooby/national_dex_gen3): the National Dex on FireRed to
  Emerald.
- [National Dex](https://github.com/sanjinpepic/gen1recomp-national-dex): the National Dex on
  Red to Crystal.
- [WoobleView](https://github.com/poooooby/gen3-hd-sprites): HD battles, Pokédex and summary.
- [NaviPlay](https://github.com/poooooby/PlayNav): Pokémon-Amie and Super Training use this
  art (on Red to Crystal, set its GEN 1/2 SPRITES to MODDED).

## 📌 Good to Know

- Can't be on together with G9 Battle Sprites.
- 🎨 Full colour everywhere needs the ADVANCED colours on Red/Blue/Yellow or GEN 2 colours on
  Gold/Silver/Crystal. Other modes show the sprites in the palette's shades, like the game's own.
- Mega and Gigantamax forms show their normal sprites; no Tera or Dynamax effects.
- On FireRed to Emerald, Castform's weather forms and the party icons of #1–386 are the game's
  own. With BATTLE SPRITES off there, battles show the game's own sprites for #1–386, and the
  pack's for the Pokémon the game has none of.
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Trainers without animated art keep the game's own: Hoenn's Elite Four, Teams Aqua and
  Magma, the Battle Frontier and some Hoenn classes. Lorelei, Agatha and Hoenn's big battles keep
  the game's own transitions. Your own character is always the game's sprite for the one you
  chose.
- 🖥️ The VS screens fill any window. Trainers slide in across the full width with WoobleView's
  Fill Screen on.
