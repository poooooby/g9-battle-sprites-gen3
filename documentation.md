# g9-battle-sprites — manifest description

This file records the full `description` for the **g9-battle-sprites** mod
(`manifest.json`). The manifest's own `description` field is capped at
**80 characters** (a studio rule, so the Mod Manager row and the generator
listing stay readable), so the complete text lives here and the manifest
carries only a short summary.

PATCH 3.1.2: the always-on frontArt(mon) export now bakes frame 1 ALONE -- trimmed to that frame's own pixels -- and answers a FOURTH value, the frame's own content box {x=0, y=0, w, h}, so a caller cropping a head space can treat the frame's top as the creature's top. An older copy trimmed the bake to the WHOLE ANIMATION's union box, so a big sheet's frame 1 sat dozens of transparent rows below that top (DRAGONITE's Mega frame has 51 spare rows; ETERNATUS' frame 1 is a 90x86 content box at y=4 inside a 111x102 union) and a card cropping from the top landed on blank rows -- which is why the party portraits of big sprites came out empty or mis-placed. A caller can tell the two apart because only the new bake answers a box; a pack that answers none keeps the old best-effort crop and the caller logs one line naming 3.1.2. LOVE 11.5 has no Image pixel readback (Image:newImageData and Image:getData do not exist there), so that content box has to come out of the bake itself rather than a scan of the decoded image; the g9ContentBox used by the HD icon atlas now scans the ImageData the atlas was decoded into for the same reason.

PATCH 3.1.3: the Dynamax/Gigantamax visuals get two additions and one rebuild. (1) The size-up is now a PHASED LADDER run on real time: a Dynamaxed/Gigantamaxed sprite grows x1, then 1.05 (0.3s), 1.10 (0.4s), 1.20 (0.5s), 1.50 (0.8s), each phase easing in on a parabolic curve (t*t) so it starts slowly and grows dramatically toward the phase's end, then shrinks back over 0.35s when the mon reverts. It scales about the sprite's bottom-centre on Gen 1 (the drawBattlerPic seam) and returns the multiplier on Gen 2 (the picScale seam). (2) The DYNAMAX CLOUD is now a CROWN: a tilted ring of eight irregular cumulus puffs over the head, with the FAR half of the ring composited BEHIND the sprite (the head occludes it) and the NEAR half in FRONT (it covers the head), so the crown reads with real depth. It cycles very slowly on a 16-second clock, and every phase is pixel-distinct because each puff carries its own size/radius/angle jitter. The ring is centred on the HEAD (the mean x of the opaque pixels in the sprite's top band), not the sheet, so an off-centre creature's crown still sits on its head. (3) New option DYNAMAX GROW (on by default) switches the size ladder independently of the cloud.

PATCH 3.1.4: the DYNAMAX CLOUD crown is rebuilt. (1) It is no longer a ring of eight redrawn puffs -- it is the cloud sheet's OWN cloud pieces. assets/dynamax_cloud.png is split at load time into its separate clouds (connected opaque masses, masses a few pixels apart merged, debris dropped), each is cropped WHOLE -- never a slice cut through a cloud -- and the sheet's THREE clouds ride the ring. (2) Geometry follows: a wider, flatter, tighter ring (ringR/tilt/drop re-tuned) with bigger pieces, so the three read as clouds on the head instead of as scattered specks, and the far half is tinted lighter than before so the red stays red. (3) The ring now turns in TWELVE baked steps instead of eight -- the three clouds are all different shapes, so every step is visibly different and a coarse step would jump -- and the per-slot jitter that used to make an even ring's turn visible is gone with it. A replacement cloud sheet needs no re-measuring: the pieces, their sizes and their spacing all come out of the sheet itself.\n\nPATCH 3.1.5: the DYNAMAX CLOUD ring is now a real tilted orbit, and its clouds no longer turn. (1) The ring is the 2D projection of a CIRCLE LEANING BACK 15 degrees from the arena floor, seen from the front -- so tilt is sin(15) and a piece's x comes off the ring's true radius while its y comes off the foreshortened one -- instead of an eyeballed oval. (2) The per-piece LEAN is gone: pieces used to be tilted about their own centre by a fraction of the cosine of their angle on the ring (rotAmt), which turned each cloud away from the angle it has in the art; now the pieces are only MOVED along the ring and never turned, so every cloud keeps exactly the shape and angle it has in assets/dynamax_cloud.png and the ring always reads as the sheet's own broken circle, whichever way round it has come. (3) The ring's room and the band the crown claims above the head are measured from a piece's own half-width and half-height (the pieces are stamped upright) rather than its half-diagonal, so the ring sits at its proper size instead of being pulled in by a bound that no longer applies.\n\nPATCH 3.1.6: the DYNAMAX CLOUD crown is now a wreath that holds at every step of its turn. (1) assets/dynamax_cloud.png is a NEW cloud sheet -- six red smoke-clouds painted round the very ellipse the crown rides, in the pack's own maroons, replacing the old sheet's two big clouds and a wisp (chunky enough that a ring of them clumped into one mass over the head). (2) The crown's clouds are now spaced EVENLY round the ring and each is TURNED to follow the ring's curve where it sits, so a cloud's body runs ALONG the ring instead of across it -- the crown reads as a ring of clouds at every step of the turn, instead of clumping whenever the art's own gaps came round to the top. (3) The ring is a wider, rounder ellipse (about 1.7 wide to 1 -- the one the reference draws) and rides just ABOVE the head, so its own middle stays open sky instead of being filled by the clouds drawn in front of the creature; the pieces are smaller and up to SIX are used, so a replacement sheet of any cloud count still builds a wreath. (4) FIXED: when two cloud masses merged at load time, every piece after the merged pair could be silently dropped -- Lua's # on a table with holes is any border it likes -- so a sheet whose wisps touch lost clouds; the survivors are now collected by count. (5) The head-band probe and the crown band follow the pieces' new aspect.

PATCH 3.2.0: the DYNAMAX CLOUD crown is REMOVED. The Dynamax visuals are now the battle screen's: while a Pokemon is Dynamaxed or Gigantamaxed it is ringed with a red aura and the field is darkened under a maroon wash, and a Dynamaxed mon that faints is held, shrinking, until its shrink-back finishes and then bursts (a Dynamax that merely expires its turns, or a switch-out, gets no burst). The size ladder stays here, and it now shrinks back at the very rates it grew -- the four grow phases in reverse, the same 2.00s down for a 1.50x mon -- instead of the old 0.35s fall; and this mod exposes its live Dynamax state (mod.exports.dynamaxStateOf) for the screen to read. The old assets/dynamax_cloud.png and the whole cloud-ring machinery are gone (the crown band, the ring phase, the tilt/ring/step constants and the CLOUD render path), so a frame is once again just the sprite plus the Tera film and the shadow. See g9-Battle-Scene's DYNAMAX DARKEN / DYNAMAX AURA rows for the new field FX. PATCH 3.2.1: TERA ART now answers a custom battle screen's `__g9TeraHold` flag on a battler as "paint no film yet", so g9-Battle-Scene's animated terastallization sequence can build its crystal construct around a creature whose tera is already live (an enemy/boss activation) and place the crystal film only when the construct BREAKS -- the reveal. With no such flag the behavior is unchanged. PATCH 3.2.2: that `__g9TeraHold` flag may now be a NUMBER -- an absolute love.timer deadline -- as well as a boolean: while it is a number the film is held only until that deadline passes, so g9-Battle-Scene can mark the battler with a self-expiring hold and the crystal film can never stay hidden forever even if the screen's reveal beat is somehow missed. A boolean `true` still means 'held until cleared', a missing/absent flag still means 'paint', and an unreadable clock degrades to 'paint' rather than hiding the film. PATCH 3.2.3: the crystal film's LIVE state is now read from the ENGINE's own record -- `mon.teraActive` plus g9-battle-engine's `getTeraType(mon)` -- instead of from battle_forms' `describe()` payload alone, and a custom battle screen may also DECLARE the type on the battler or through the draw seam (`liveTeraType`). battle_forms' payload was not a shape this mod can rely on across builds, which is why a Terastallized Pokemon could draw with NO crystal layer at all; the engine read is the very value its combat code uses, and battle_forms' describe() is still the fallback (along with the declared raid boss path, unchanged). PATCH 3.2.4: the crystal pattern's loader (loadPattern) is RESTORED -- it was called by beginBuild but never defined anywhere, so the film bake threw and every type sheet silently fell back to its plain bake, which is why the crystal layer never appeared at all; the pattern now decodes lazily through the sheet loader's own three routes (path, raw bytes, Image), caches, and a missing or unreadable asset warns once and bakes plain rather than failing. PATCH 3.2.5: the Dynamax grow ladder now honours the battle screen's staging hold (`battler.__g9DynHold`) -- `dynamaxActive` answers false while it is up, so a pre-activated Dynamax is drawn ordinary-sized until the screen's own clip reaches its reveal beat, exactly as the Tera film's `__g9TeraHold` works. This keeps the size change from showing through the transformation animation when a gimmick fires on the same turn. PATCH 3.2.6: the always-on frontArt(mon) export now hands its portrait flag the value it was always meant to. That export trimmed a bake to frame 1 when the caller asked for a portrait -- a card cropping a head space wants one pose, not the whole animation's union box -- but its call into the internal baker passed SIXTEEN arguments to a FIFTEEN-parameter function, so every value from the fifth onward shifted by one and the trailing true was discarded: portrait silently received nil and every bake came back as the union box. Big sheets looked right because their frame 1 nearly fills the union; a species whose frame 1 is much smaller than its animation -- TOTODILE's 37x36 pose inside a 43x59 union, GASTLY's 40x20 inside a 40x50, anything drawn small on a large canvas -- got a portrait card cropped to the union's top, which is transparent rows, so the START/POKeMON roster showed a blank or empty card for exactly those species. The extra argument is gone: the trailing true now lands on portrait and the bake is frame 1 trimmed to itself, as it was always documented to be. The studio harness gains a check that bakes a sheet whose frames grow (union far taller than frame 1) and asserts the portrait bake is frame 1's own box on both generations, so this class of argument-shift bug reproduces there instead of only in-game. Callers on g9-gui 2.6.7 or later get correct head crops for small species; the frame's own content box (3.1.2) is unchanged. PATCH 3.3.0: EGGS get the pack's own picture, everywhere an egg is shown. A Pokemon egg has no species sheet and no icon art of its own, so every egg is now drawn from ONE new still image shipped with the mod -- assets/egg.png, La Base de Sky's Graphics/Pokemon/Eggs/000.png (LA BASE DE SKY) trimmed to its own 56x60 pixels. That one picture is handed out through the same always-on exports the pack's sheets use: frontArt(mon) answers (image, w, h, box) for an egg, so g9-gui's party portrait cards and its PC mon panel show the egg and it stands in as the 'battle sprite' asset even though an egg can never be dragged into a battle; iconArt(mon)/iconArtHD(mon)/iconArt16(mon) all answer the egg, so a party row draws it -- fitted into the 16x16 slot on the classic party list, near its own pixels in the 64px cell of G9 PARTY SCREEN; and drawSummaryFrame(mon, box) paints it, so the summary's own picture slot shows the egg too. An egg is a still, so it is never sliced into animation frames and never goes through the sheet baker, and a build whose assets/egg.png is missing warns once and keeps the pack's old atlas '000' egg icon. To change the egg art later: drop a new assets/egg.png in (any size -- callers fit it by its longer side) and nothing else has to move.

PATCH 3.3.1: a battle send-out's sheet now reaches the screen as soon as the ball lands, and a partially-downloaded file no longer pins a species to the vanilla pic. (1) HOT BUILDING: a sheet a caller is actively asking for (a send-out, a POKeDEX page, a summary) is marked hot and stepped FIRST, under a larger per-update budget, until the asks stop -- so a background bake can never spend the frame budget while a mon is about to land. (2) PROGRESSIVE FRAMES: the still-growing frame array is handed out as soon as frame 1 is baked, so a first-look send-out animates in from its first frame instead of holding a blank pic until the whole sheet is done. (3) BOUNDED RETRIES: a sheet that is present but fails to decode (the common real case -- a PNG caught mid-write by a download still in flight) is re-read a few times before it is latched as unusable, so a file that lands a moment later is picked up instead of being vanilla for the rest of the session (the Farfetch'd symptom). A file that is genuinely missing is unaffected -- it is still only re-checked every ~15s. The g9-Battle-Scene from 4.0.2 also pre-warms BOTH leads the moment a battle screen is built, so a send-out's art gets the whole intro as head start.

PATCH 3.3.2: the DECLARED Dynamax/Gigantamax read now honours the custom battle screen's `__g9DynHold` staging flag, the same hold the LIVE read has always honoured. A wild raid boss's Dynamax is a DECLARATION (g9-Battle-Scene's `g9RaidGimmick`), and g9-Battle-Scene 4.0.4 plays that boss's own grow sequence as an intro beat: it raises `__g9DynHold` while the clip runs and drops it on the reveal beat. Without this the declared path started the size ladder the moment the boss appeared, so the creature crept up to full size underneath the whole transformation being shown over it. The hold is only ever up mid-sequence (the scene clears it at the reveal, at the end of the intro, and whenever a beat cannot be staged), so a declared boss drawn outside a sequence is unaffected -- and so is every other path, since nothing else sets the flag.

DOCS ONLY (no version bump): g9-gui 3.2.2 draws its 'ICONS' party portraits from this pack's own 64x64 HD cells (`iconArtHD`) at their real 1:1 size, and keeps the 16x16 atlas (`iconArt16`) only as the fitted fallback for a copy of this mod that has no HD cell. The 16x16 atlas is a 4:1 point-sampled copy of those HD frames, so the atlas path drew every party icon at HALF the pack's own pixel scale and read as a shrunken figure in the portrait card; g9-gui now crops the HD cell to the card through the same head-space rule its front sheets use. The `iconArtHD` entry above already advertised the HD cell as what g9-gui's roster draws, but the `iconArt16` entry and the two cross-reference sentences (the export section in README.md and mod.card) still said g9-gui preferred the 16x16 atlas, so they are corrected here. No export, option or engine behaviour changes: both exports stay always-on, and a copy of g9-gui older than 3.2.2 sees exactly what it saw before.

FEATURE 3.4.0: the two always-on icon exports now take an OPTIONAL second argument -- a 0-based FRAME INDEX -- so a caller can pick one of the two frames the pack ships per icon instead of the live one: `iconArtHD(mon, frame)` and `iconArt16(mon, frame)` return that exact frame's quad table and cell, and `iconArtHD` returns THAT frame's own opaque-pixel box with it. Calling with no frame (or nil) keeps the old live-frame behaviour, so every existing caller -- including an older g9-gui -- sees exactly what it saw before. g9-gui 3.3.0 uses the parameter for its PARTY PORTRAITS animation (the ICONS-only PORTRAIT ANIMATION row): it steps the two frames once a second on its own wall clock and takes each frame's own box, so the portrait crop stays put while the creature bobs. Before this the icons could only follow this mod's sprite FPS (or hold frame 0), and a caller holding one frame while reading the live frame's box would have made the crop jitter.

FEATURE 3.5.0: the gen-1 Pokemon Tower ghost is left to the engine. Until the SILPH SCOPE is in the bag, every wild battle in the tower -- and the scripted ghost MAROWAK -- is disguised as a "GHOST": the engine swaps the enemy's name and pic for its own assets/generated/battle/front/ghost.png and keeps the real mon hidden behind that picture (BattleState.makeGhost without the Scope, makeUnveiledGhost with it), and the pack must NOT paint its sheet over that disguise -- being unable to tell what is underneath is the whole encounter. The gen-1 `drawPicsLayer` wrap now skips the enemy substitution whenever the engine's own disguise bookkeeping is up -- `battle.ghost` (no Scope), `battle.scopeReveal` (the Scope is held and the reveal is playing) or `battle.ghostReveal` -- and the `resolveBattleScale` wrap hands that pic the engine's own scale instead of the managed 1 (the ghost is the engine's palette-processed pic, not one of our pre-baked frames), with no true-colour mark. The read is deliberately SPECIES-BLIND: a randomizer may replace whatever the disguise hides (g9-battle-sample randomizes the tower's wilds too, and any other randomizer may), so keying on Marowak -- or any species -- would both miss a randomized ghost and paint over an ordinary Marowak fought elsewhere. `ghostReal` is deliberately NOT read: the disguise sets it and nothing ever clears it, so it outlives a Scope reveal and would wrongly keep the sheet out after the real mon has been unmasked. The guard lasts the one draw call, so an ordinary battle for the same species still gets the pack's frame and the managed scale. Gen-1 only: Gold's facade has no makeGhost, and no layout battle scene runs the scope-less ghost (the sample scene bails on `battle.ghost` on purpose), while with the Scope held the mon is identified and the scene draws it normally.

PATCH 3.5.1: frontArt answers a FIFTH value, `head` — the height, in the frame image's own rows, of a leading head DECORATION in the frame (a blade, a leek, a hilt), or 0 when the frame's top IS the head — so a caller can start its portrait window BELOW the decoration and show the face instead of the decoration. This is what fixes the party-portrait cards for Kingambit, Sirfetchd and Aegislash, whose frames are dominated at the top by their blade/leek/hilt: g9-gui 3.8.1 reads the value and slides its window down by it. The height is measured from the ART, never a species list: the portrait bake already records every source row's own opaque width, and a leading run of rows is a decoration when the first row that reaches a QUARTER of the creature's widest row lies below a run that is taller than it is wide (a vertical spike) AND is at least a FIFTH of the frame's own height. Both guards matter. The spike test is what keeps genuinely TALL creatures out — their head reaches a real share of the width within a few rows, so Eternatus (head 33 of 89 px by row 12), Lapras (26 of 62 by row 2), Wailord (52 of 86 by row 16), Dragonite, Milotic, Lugia (13 rows above a 20-wide crown), Kyurem, Samurott, Zacian, Giratina and Alakazam all answer 0. The size floor is what keeps short narrow TIPS out: Pikachu's 6-row 5px ear tips and Onix's 7-row 4px rock tip are taller than they are wide, but they are not "large head decorations" and sliding the window for them would crop the head of a Pokemon whose top IS its head. Measured against the real pack the rule fires on exactly Sirfetchd (37 rows, 42% of the frame), Kingambit (29 rows, 32%) and Aegislash's hilt (17 rows, 21%, on both its forms) and on nothing else out of 121 front sheets. An older copy of this mod answers four values, so the value is nil and every caller keeps the old top-anchored crop; a caller that ignores the extra value sees no change at all.

PATCH 3.6.0: national_dex moves from `dependencies` to `optional_dependencies` — this mod has NO hard dependency. It never called national_dex's API (its form map is a generated join in data/dbk_data.lua, and every lookup is keyed off a live mon or a dex entry the game is showing), so it already ran on a cart without the dex: species resolve against whatever dex the RUNNING game has, a species only national_dex knows simply never appears in play, and a species the pack has no sheet for keeps the vanilla pic. This release only makes the manifest say so.
